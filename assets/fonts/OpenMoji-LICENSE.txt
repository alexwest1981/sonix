Why it is bundled
-----------------
Sonix draws ~200 icons as emoji characters. Systems normally ship only
"Noto Color Emoji", which is a CBDT bitmap font that egui's rasterizer
(ab_glyph) cannot use — every icon then renders as an empty box. OpenMoji-black
is a plain monochrome TrueType font and covers every icon the app uses.
